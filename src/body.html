01 About

Most sites look fine and do nothing. I care about the other kind — the ones that load fast, rank, convert, and still feel like someone made them. Strategy, design, code and the boring infrastructure underneath: same person, start to finish.

What I actually do

I design and build websites and web products — WordPress and Next.js — then handle the parts most designers hand off: SEO and structured data, analytics, lead capture, performance, security and long-term maintenance.

How I work

Direct. You talk to the person doing the work. Fixed scope, real timelines, no account-manager telephone game. Everything I build is documented and editable by your team afterwards.

Who I work with

Founders, small teams and agencies in Canada and India — real estate, news publishing, healthcare, procurement, home services and SaaS. From one landing page to a fleet of sites.

I want to build something that ends up in a museum. Until then I’ll settle for building something that ends up making you money.

05 Process

Five steps.No mystery.

Step 01

Discover

A call, your goals, your competitors and the constraints nobody mentions until week three. You get a written scope and a fixed price before anything starts.

Step 02

Design

Direction first, then real screens — not a mood board and a promise. You see the actual homepage early and we iterate until it is right.

Step 03

Build

Clean, editable, fast. Built on the stack that fits your team, with SEO, analytics and lead capture wired in from the first commit rather than the last week.

Step 04

Launch

Staging, QA across devices, redirects, schema, Search Console, backups and a rollback plan. Launch day should be boring — that is the whole point.

Step 05

Grow

Updates, monitoring, security and the ongoing optimisation that turns a launched site into a compounding asset. Or a clean handover, if you would rather run it yourself.
