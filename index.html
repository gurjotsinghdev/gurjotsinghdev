01 About

Most sites look fine and do nothing. I care about the other kind. The ones that load fast, rank, convert, and still feel like someone made them. Strategy, design, code and the boring infrastructure underneath: same person, start to finish.

What I actually do

I work with companies to build websites and to run the marketing campaigns that bring people to them. Alongside that I provide IT consultancy and support of every kind: hosting and servers, security, integrations, automation and the day-to-day help that keeps a business running.

How I work

Direct. You talk to the person doing the work. Fixed scope, real timelines, no account-manager telephone game. Everything I build is documented and editable by your team afterwards.

Who I work with

Founders, businesses and agencies across Canada. Restaurants, real estate, trades and home services, healthcare, professional services, construction, ecommerce, publishing, nonprofits and manufacturing. From one landing page to a fleet of sites.

I want to build something that ends up in a museum. Until then I’ll settle for building something that helps your business grow.
